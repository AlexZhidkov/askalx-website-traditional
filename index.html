Frequently Asked Questions

  • What can an AI Agent do for my business?

    Asking what an AI agent can do for you is like asking what a human employee can do for your business. The answer depends entirely on what you need them to do. These agents are fully customizable and can learn to do practically anything on your computer.

  • How do I get started with implementing an AI assistant?

    You get started by initialising your AI Agent by providing it information about your business, your goals and ambitions. In other words, you need to give it your business context and your intent. Once initialised, you can use a process called reverse prompting to define its role and capabilities. Sample prompt: "Based on what you know about me and my goals, what information I can provide to you in order for you to be able to help me achieve my goals faster and take as much off my plate as possible". When you finish, ask the assistant, "What are five high lever tasks you can do right now to get us closer to our goals?" to identify initial workflows.
